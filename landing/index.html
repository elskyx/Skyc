Workflow

Three Steps. Zero Waiting.

Engineered to be completely unobtrusive. You don't even have to open the app manually on Android.

01

Copy Link or Tap Share

Copy any media link from TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, or Spotify. On Android, simply tap Share inside any app and select Skyc for a floating quick-save overlay.

02

Instant Client-Side Parse

Skyc's built-in JavaScript engine executes scraper modules directly on your device CPU. Tokens are resolved, watermarks stripped, and CDN signatures unlocked in under 200ms.

03

Native Storage & Gallery

Files are streamed directly into your operating system's standard directories. On Android, videos instantly index into Google Photos / Gallery. On Windows, they organize cleanly in your media library.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Skyc, security, platform compatibility, and updates.

Is Skyc really 100% free with no catch?

Yes. There is no catch, no VIP tier, and no advertisement. Skyc is an open-source hobby project distributed under the GNU General Public License v3.0 (GPL-3.0). Because it runs client-side on your own device, there are no expensive middleman servers to monetize.

How does Zero-Cloud client-side downloading work?

Traditional downloader websites make you paste a link, send it to their server, scrape the file on their machine, and then re-stream it to you. Skyc embeds the scraper algorithms directly inside the application runtime. Your device talks directly to the official content delivery network (CDN) without any intermediary.

What happens when a platform updates its code and breaks a scraper?

Skyc features an integrated Over-The-Air (OTA) Hot-Patching engine. When TikTok, Instagram, or YouTube alter their token signatures, the community updates the scraper bundle in the official GitHub repository. Skyc downloads this micro-bundle silently on app launch, restoring functionality without requiring full APK or .exe reinstallations.

Where do downloaded media files get saved on my device?

On Android, files are organized into standard system folders: Movies/Skyc for videos, Music/Skyc for audio, and Pictures/Skyc for images and PDFs, instantly recognized by your Gallery. On Windows, files save to your user profile's default Videos\Skyc and Music\Skyc folders.

How does the Instant Photo-to-PDF feature work?

When you download an Instagram carousel post or a multi-page TikTok photo story, Skyc allows you to either save individual JPG files or compile them sequentially into a clean, paginated PDF document. This is ideal for saving comic chapters, design slides, and educational infographics.

Can I inspect or build Skyc from source code?

Absolutely. The complete source code, build scripts, scraper compilations, and release pipelines are published transparently on GitHub (elskyx/Skyc). Pull requests and scraper enhancements are always welcome!
